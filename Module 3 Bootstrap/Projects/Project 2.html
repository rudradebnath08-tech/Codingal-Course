Ride Dictionary

Roller Coaster
A high-speed thrill ride that launches you over glowing loops and plunges through clouds of mist.
Treasure Trail
A mysterious forest quest full of puzzles, secret clues, and hidden rewards for the fastest teams.
River Rush
A splashy water adventure that twists through canyon walls and sprays riders with cool, refreshing droplets.

Safety Mission

Please assemble at Gate 3 before the challenge begins for a quick explorer briefing and map.

The rescue drill will happen near River Zone, where teams practice river safety under glowing lanterns.

Ride Commands

Start the coaster: ride.start("Roller Coaster")

Open the trail: trail.open("Treasure Trail")

Stop the ride: ride.stop("River Rush")
