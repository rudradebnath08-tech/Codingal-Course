My Fitness Routine Cards

This page shows a simple weekly fitness routine. Each card has its own content area, padding, border, and margin.

Morning Stretch

Start the day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck.

Keep your body flexible and ready for the day ahead.

Time: 10 minutes

Cardio Workout

Do jumping jacks, jogging, or skipping to improve stamina and energy.

Try to keep your heart rate up for a good cardiovascular workout.

Time: 20 minutes

Strength Training

Practise squats, push-ups, and planks to build body strength.

Do 3 sets of 10 repetitions for each exercise.

Time: 15 minutes

Yoga Time

Try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and focus.

Do 3-5 poses and hold each for 30 seconds.

Time: 15 minutes

Evening Walk

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind.

Remember to maintain a steady pace and enjoy the surroundings.

Time: 25 minutes

Rest and Hydrate

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

Do not skip meals or compromise on sleep quality.

Time: All day

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.

Stay consistent with your routine for the best results.
