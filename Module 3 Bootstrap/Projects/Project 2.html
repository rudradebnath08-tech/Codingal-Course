Ride Dictionary

Roller Coaster
A fast ride that takes visitors high above the park and then zooms down at high speed.
Treasure Trail
A mystery path where visitors solve clues in groups to find treasure. Whichever group finds it first, gets to keep it!
River Rush
A water ride that moves quickly through twists and turns. We Recommend riders to wear life jackets.

Safety Mission

Please report to Gate 3 before the challenge begins.

The rescue drill will happen near River Zone.

Ride Commands

Start the coaster: ride.start("Roller Coaster")

Open the trail: trail.open("Treasure Trail")

Stop the ride: ride.stop("River Rush")

Park Short Forms

APST checks every ride before opening.

RRU helps visitors near water rides.

JEC is for young adventure lovers.
